About Us

Welcome to Fort Hotel Yasin

Standing at the heart of Yasin Valley, Fort Hotel Yasin blends traditional and modern architecture — dark stone walls, warm timber and a wide front lawn under the Hindu Kush foothills.

Guests describe the rooms as spacious and clean, the food as proper local cooking, and the staff as friendly and helpful. Travellers crossing the Ghizer valleys — and trekking groups heading for Darkot — have made it their Yasin stop for years.

Yasin Valley, From Our Door

One of Ghizer's quieter valleys — old forts, terraced villages and high passes, reached by the side road from Gupis.

The ruins of Yasin Fort sit near the town — a reminder of the valley's days as a small principality. From the hotel, days out run up-valley to Barkulti and Darkot village, the last stop below the historic Darkot Pass into Chitral's Broghil Valley.

Tour routes through Yasin also visit Muduri Fort and the Mazar of Lalak Jan, while the villages between offer easy walks among terraced fields, orchards and glacier-fed streams.

Yasin is about six hours by road from Gilgit, turning off the main Ghizer road at Gupis. The high passes are best attempted from July to September, with a local guide.
